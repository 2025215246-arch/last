Marker: Use the standard Hiro marker: open / print Hiro marker. Keep the marker flat, well-lit, and fully visible to the camera.

What is included

01 · PCBGreen board, main microcontroller, USB connector and pin headers.
02 · ComponentsResistors, LEDs, capacitors, switch and metal leads.
03 · Jumper wiresRaised red, blue and yellow wire paths.

When the AR scene opens, the camera view may take a few seconds to initialize. If prompted, allow camera permission. This demo builds its geometry in the browser and does not require a separate GLB file.
